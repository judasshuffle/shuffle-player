Musical styles and search tips

Try play 70s Bob Dylan: artist matches from 1970–1979. Commands never start playback automatically. Two-digit decades 00s–20s mean 2000–2029; 30s–90s mean 1930–1999. Use four digits to avoid ambiguity.

Choose a broad style or one of your library’s subgenres, then Play matches or Queue matches. Try play blues, play genre disco, or play 70s genre jazz. Use play artist Pop to search an artist whose name is also a style.
