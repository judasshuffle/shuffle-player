    Connect DarkRide: start here

    First get listening working at home. Then choose whether people outside your home should be able to listen. The preview and live player are separate stations.

    1. Hear it at home

    An address beginning 192.168. or 10. is usually a home-network address. It can work on your Wi-Fi without working anywhere else.

    Local listening has not been tested in this browser.

    Open this player's screen for audio, visuals and lighting analysis at home.

    2. Set up listening outside your home

    Your public address takes a visitor to your router. Port forwarding tells the router which device runs your player. Typing a public address below does not set up the router for you.

    Show me what goes in the router

    Open your router's Port Forwarding or Virtual Servers page. Use TCP and forward one chosen outside port to your player's listening-only port.

    It still doesn't connect — what should I check?

    Check that your public domain points to your router, the TCP port matches the forwarding rule, and the device firewall allows the listening port. Two routers may both need forwarding.

    If your provider uses a shared public address (CGNAT), ordinary port forwarding may not work. Ask your provider whether you have a public IP that accepts incoming connections. This is a connection limitation, not something you did wrong.
